.. py:attribute:: anchor
   :type: 'center' | 'n' | 'ne' | 'e' | 'se' | 's' | 'sw' | 'w' | 'nw'

   It controls where the text is positioned if the widget has more space than the text needs.

   ``"center"`` places it in the middle of the widget, ``"n"`` in the center attached to the top edge,
   and ``"sw"`` positions the text in the lower-left corner of the available space.
